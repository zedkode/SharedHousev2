# SharedHouse Member Guide

## What the app does

SharedHouse helps people living together understand what is due, what has been paid, what needs doing and who is responsible. It records household information; it does not move money or prove that a bank transfer settled.

## Joining a house

Install/open the app and create or sign in to your verified account. Choose **Join with invitation**,
paste the private code, review the household name, role and expiry, then confirm. If the invitation is
restricted to an email address, you must be signed in with that exact address. You can join another
home later and switch the active household from the Household tab.

Invitation codes are currently shared manually; clickable app links and automatic invitation email
are not available yet. Set your language, appearance, accessibility and notification choices in
Settings. The tutorial can be skipped on first use and replayed later from Settings.

Never forward a private invite link unless the household administrator intended it to be shared. Report an invitation you do not recognise.

## Home dashboard

The dashboard shows your outstanding amount for the current cycle, amounts you recorded as paid, upcoming due items, your next chores and important household updates. Tap any total to see exactly which bills, adjustments and payment records created it.

## Bills and rent

Open **Money** to view rent, electricity, gas, internet and other shared costs. Each item shows the
authoritative total, your share, due date, status and split explanation. Use the filters for approved,
proposed, reversed or all entries. In the current release, a new expense is split equally between
all active household members; the detail sheet shows the exact allocation before you rely on it.

Members can submit a proposal. An owner or administrator must approve it before it contributes to
the approved summaries. Read-only members can inspect the ledger but cannot add or change entries.

Active household-cost templates appear under **Planned household costs**. Choose **Use** to prefill
the configured title, category, price and next due date, then check and save the real expense.
Templates may use standard categories or an administrator-defined label. A template is a plan, not
proof that a bill was issued or paid.

### Marking an amount paid

Payment declarations are planned but are not available in the current Android release. SharedHouse
does not move money and must not be treated as proof that a bank transfer was made; continue to
verify transfers in your bank account.

To correct a mistake, an eligible user chooses **Reverse ledger entry** and provides a reason.
History remains visible so totals stay trustworthy; entries are never silently deleted.

## Shopping

Use the shared list to add quantity, expected price and notes. Claim an item when you plan to buy it. Mark unavailable or substitute when needed. After purchase, enter the actual price and optionally upload a receipt. If reimbursement is shared, the app shows who is included before creating the household charge.

## Chores

Your assignments appear on Home, Tasks and Calendar with your avatar. Open a task for instructions, time window and completion requirements.

- **Complete:** finish and add a note/photo only if required.
- **Request help:** keep responsibility but ask another member to assist.
- **Request swap:** ask an eligible member to take responsibility.
- **Postpone:** choose an allowed later time and state a reason.
- **Report issue:** flag missing supplies, unsafe condition or wrong assignment.

A request is pending until accepted or resolved.

## Calendar

Switch between agenda, week and month. Filter money, chores, shopping, events or only your items. Financial events show amount/status; chores show assigned avatars. Recurring items clearly ask whether a change applies to one occurrence or more.

## Notifications

Choose categories, reminder lead time, quiet hours, sound/vibration and lock-screen detail. High-priority household alerts are still ordinary app notifications, not official emergency warnings, and can be disabled except narrowly required security/service messages.

## Appearance and language

Choose light, dark or system appearance. Android may offer device dynamic colour. The first language follows your phone when English/Romanian is available, and you can change it any time.

## Privacy and account

View active devices, revoke sessions, request your data export and start account deletion in Settings. If you own a household, transfer ownership or close it first. Cancelling/deleting the app account does not automatically cancel an Apple/Google subscription; use the subscription-management link shown in the app.

## Getting help

Use in-app Help to open role-appropriate articles or contact support. Include record IDs/screenshots only when necessary and never send passwords or verification codes.
