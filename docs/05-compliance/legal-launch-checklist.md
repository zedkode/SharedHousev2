# Legal and Compliance Launch Checklist

This checklist identifies decisions requiring evidence. “Complete” means an accountable owner has approved the item and retained the supporting artefact.

## Corporate and product

- Operating legal entity and consumer contracting party confirmed.
- Product/brand trademark and domain checks completed.
- Terms of service reviewed for UK, EEA and US launch scope.
- Product wording does not promise payment settlement, legal debt proof or emergency-service status.
- Minimum age and eligibility policy approved.

## Privacy

- Data map, processing purposes and lawful-basis record completed.
- Controller/processor/vendor roles documented.
- Privacy notice and just-in-time disclosures approved in English and Romanian.
- UK/EU rights workflow, US state-rights matrix and appeal process configured.
- Data retention schedule approved.
- International transfer mechanisms and assessments completed.
- Vendor DPAs, subprocessors and security reviews completed.
- DPIA or equivalent screening completed; full assessment performed where required.
- Incident/breach response and notification decision process approved.

## Mobile stores

- Apple App Privacy and privacy manifests verified against final binary/SDKs.
- Google Data safety declarations verified against final binary/SDKs.
- Account deletion works in-app and on required web route.
- Subscription disclosures, restore and management flows tested.
- Apple/Google server notification verification and reconciliation tested.
- Current SDK, target API and billing-library rules checked on submission date.
- Review account uses synthetic data and all features are reachable.

## Consumer and commerce

- Prices, taxes, subscription periods, renewal and cancellation wording reviewed.
- Refund/support policy aligns with store/provider rules and consumer law.
- Marketing claims and trial/offer terms substantiated.
- Accessibility statement/support route prepared.
- Web purchase flow, if enabled, receives separate store-policy and payments review.

## Security and operations

- Threat model and external penetration test completed.
- Production access, MFA, audit and break-glass controls reviewed.
- Backups/restoration and deletion propagation tested.
- Incident contacts, on-call and status communication are operational.
- Vulnerability reporting channel and security contact published.

## Explicit pre-launch legal review triggers

- Holding or transmitting tenant money.
- Open-banking/payment initiation.
- Debt collection, credit, deposits or rent guarantees.
- Landlord/tenant legal workflows or automated legal advice.
- Children or school/family accounts.
- Biometric identity, precise location or contact-book collection.
- Behavioural advertising, sale/sharing or data brokerage.
- AI decisions that materially affect members.
- Public social/community features.
