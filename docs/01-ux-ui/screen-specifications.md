# Screen Specifications

## Authentication and invitation

### Invitation landing

Shows inviter, household name, invite expiry, proposed role, privacy notice link and actions to sign in or create account. It does not reveal member lists or financial data before authentication.

### Account creation

Email, password or passwordless path, terms/privacy acknowledgement, age confirmation (18+), email verification and optional marketing consent kept separate from service consent.

## Onboarding

1. Welcome and product value.
2. Language detected from device; English/Romanian override.
3. Profile name and avatar.
4. Household summary, currency and timezone confirmation.
5. How personal totals work.
6. How payment declaration/checklist works.
7. Calendar and assigned-avatar explanation.
8. Chore help/swap/postpone actions.
9. Notification category choices.
10. Finish on Home with contextual coach marks.

## Home

Displays household switcher, current cycle, personal due total, paid amount, next due item, next task, pending requests and announcements. Administrator-only household totals are visually separated from the member’s personal balance.

## Expense detail

Displays amount, category, supplier, service period, due date, split method, included members, personal share, payment declarations, evidence, revision history and actions based on capability.

## Record payment

Bottom sheet with amount, date, method, reference/evidence and confirmation choice. The final button states “Record payment” rather than “Pay” because the app does not move money.

## Calendar

Month cells show compact event markers and assigned avatars. Selecting a day opens an agenda. Selecting an event opens detail with permitted actions. Filters persist per device.

## Chore detail

Shows instructions, zone, due window, assigned members, recurrence, completion evidence requirement, history and actions: start, complete, request help, swap, postpone, report issue.

## Shopping

Lists are collaborative and real-time. Items show quantity, expected price, assignee, state and substitute notes. Purchase recording can create a reimbursement proposal.

## Household members

Avatar, name, role/capabilities, membership status and current task load. Financial details are limited to what household policy and permissions permit. Member removal warns about open tasks, pending splits and ownership transfer.

## Settings

Profile, avatar, language, theme, dynamic colours, accessibility, notifications, quiet hours, devices, security, privacy, export, delete account, subscription, help and legal.

## Platform admin web screens

- operational overview;
- users/accounts with privacy-minimised metadata;
- households metadata without default content access;
- products, plans and regional prices;
- Apple/Google transaction and webhook reconciliation;
- entitlements and overrides with expiry/reason;
- promotions and offer-code mapping;
- support cases;
- privacy/export/deletion queue;
- security events and audit explorer;
- feature flags and release controls;
- localisation/content management;
- service health and incident controls.
