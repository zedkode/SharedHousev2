# Store Submission Plan

## 1. Eight-to-twelve weeks before submission

- Verify current Apple/Google SDK, target API, billing and policy requirements.
- Freeze high-risk SDK additions and review privacy manifests/data collection.
- Finalise legal entity, contracts, privacy notice, terms and support/deletion URLs.
- Create product IDs, pricing, offers and sandbox tester accounts.
- Begin external security and accessibility review.

## 2. Four-to-eight weeks before

- Complete full purchase lifecycle and provider notification reconciliation.
- Complete export/account deletion and owner-transfer flows.
- Run internal Android and TestFlight pilots with synthetic plus consented pilot data.
- Prepare localized store copy, screenshots, app icon and review instructions.
- Complete Apple App Privacy and Google Data safety draft from code/SDK inventory.

## 3. Two-to-four weeks before

- Feature freeze except release blockers.
- Perform release-candidate device/accessibility/localisation matrix.
- Rehearse production migration, backup restoration and incident rollback.
- Verify reviewer account, invitation flow and backend availability.
- Reconcile final binary permissions/SDKs against declarations.

## 4. Submission

- Submit signed build, metadata, privacy policy, support and deletion links.
- Provide clear notes explaining household invite, synthetic review house, subscription, payment-declaration semantics and absence of money transmission.
- Staff support/engineering coverage during review.

## 5. Rollout

Use phased/staged release. Monitor crashes, sign-in, invites, sync, ledger integrity, notification jobs, purchase verification, provider-event age and deletion workflows. Define numeric pause criteria before rollout.

## 6. Rejection handling

Record exact guideline/policy feedback, reproduce reviewer path, correct root cause and update evidence/docs. Do not implement misleading review-only behaviour or disable disclosed features only for review.
